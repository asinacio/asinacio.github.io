Science communication is one of the parts of being a physicist that I find most rewarding. I enjoy finding different ways to make difficult ideas accessible, whether I am speaking with a scientist, teaching a classroom of students, engaging with the general public, or exploring physics through a hands-on activity with children.

Selected outreach

  • Oxford Open Doors — Annual public-engagement activities at the University of Oxford, introducing visitors to experimental particle physics and the research carried out in the Physics Department.
  • Crater Art — Lead demonstrator for a hands-on workshop during a visit by children from the Blackbird Leys Adventure Playground to the University of Oxford Physics Department.
  • Community Stargazing — Volunteer at the particle-physics stand during a community stargazing event at Blackbird Leys College, engaging with children and families.
  • Royal Society STEM Research Careers Event — Guest at an event organised with In2scienceUK, supporting young people from disadvantaged backgrounds in exploring STEM careers.
  • Cartas com Ciência — Correspondent in a programme connecting scientists and students in Portuguese-speaking countries through an exchange of letters.
  • From the Universe to the Laboratory — Public sessions for secondary-school students at LIP-Lisbon, including Partículas: do Universo ao Laboratório and Das profundezas da Terra ao Espaço.
02 Digital & media

Physics does not have to stay inside the paper.

I am interested in communicating science through formats that are a little less traditional than a lecture or a publication. Over the years, this has included digital content, social media, and documentary work.

Maria Ramos e Manuel Valadares

Documentary produced and edited for the Portuguese Physics Society, exploring the lives and scientific work of two Portuguese scientists of the twentieth century.

I am always interested in opportunities to talk about physics, visit schools, contribute to public-engagement activities, or work on new ways of communicating science.
