PROJECT / 01

Low-Energy Neutrino Physics

Solar neutrinos provide a unique opportunity to study both neutrino properties and the processes taking place in the interior of the Sun.

Their detection has played a central role in establishing neutrino flavour oscillations and continues to provide important tests of neutrino physics and solar models.

My interests include the study of low-energy solar neutrinos with liquid-scintillator detectors, with a particular focus on extracting weak neutrino signals from challenging backgrounds and improving the sensitivity of future measurements.

I am also interested in using solar neutrinos to search for exotic phenomena, such as solar antineutrinos, that could provide clues about new neutrino interactions or physics beyond the Standard Model.

PROJECT / 02

Neutrinoless Double-Beta Decay

Neutrinoless double-beta decay is one of the most sensitive experimental probes of the fundamental nature of the neutrino.

Its observation would demonstrate that lepton number is not conserved, provide evidence that neutrinos are Majorana particles, and offer insight into the origin and scale of neutrino masses.

Within the SNO+ experiment, I have worked on the study and modelling of radioactive backgrounds, detector calibration, and performance studies relevant to the search.

Neutrinoless double-beta decay SNO+ Background modelling Calibration
